Our Purpose

Why IDEA-South Sudan Exists

We believe that lasting peace and development in South Sudan must be driven by its youth. IDEA-South Sudan provides a platform for young people to lead peacebuilding initiatives, deliver humanitarian support, and drive community transformation. Through inclusive dialogue, partnership, and action, we are building a more resilient and united society.

Who We Are

Youth-Led Humanitarian & Development

IDEA-South Sudan is a national, youth-led non-profit organization based in Bor Town, Jonglei State, with operations in Juba. We work across humanitarian, development, and peacebuilding sectors, bringing together young leaders, civil society, and community members to foster peace and sustainable development.

Mission

Empower youth and communities through peacebuilding, humanitarian assistance, and sustainable development.

Vision

A peaceful, resilient South Sudan where youth drive positive change.

Values

Integrity, Collaboration, Inclusivity, Youth Empowerment, Peace.

Focus Areas

Our Programs

Peacebuilding

Promoting peaceful coexistence, dialogue, reconciliation, and conflict resolution.

Humanitarian Assistance

Providing emergency relief and support to communities affected by crisis and displacement.

Community Development

Strengthening local infrastructure, livelihoods, and social services.

Youth Empowerment

Training, mentorship, and leadership opportunities for young South Sudanese.

Advocacy & Engagement

Advocating for youth rights, gender equality, and inclusive participation in governance.
